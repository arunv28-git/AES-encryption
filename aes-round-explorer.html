NIST FIPS PUB 197 Mathematical Specification

In AES-128, the input 16-byte block is mapped column-by-column into a 4×4 byte matrix. Rounds 1–9 execute all four transformations. In Round 10, MixColumns is omitted to ensure cryptographic and architectural symmetry with Inverse MixColumns.
